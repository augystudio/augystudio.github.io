I had a bike
It fell apart
The tires were flat
and I didn't fix them for months.
It was a minor inconvenience,
I mostly stayed home.

My broken bike was sad
for months, under my window.

Once during that time
I went downstairs
to have a look at it
and see if I know how to fix it.
I didn't fix it.
I didn't try too hard.

My broken bike was sad
for months, under my window.

There are many bike thefts here
but who would want to steal
this old, broken bike?
So I only had one flimsy lock on it. 
And no one stole it.
Until they did.

My broken bike was sad
for months, under my window.
